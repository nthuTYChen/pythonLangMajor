Thank you for the revised invitation. I can confirm that I will attend the meeting on Tuesday, August 4, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. in Meeting Room 302. The new time works well for me.

I have begun preparing several ideas for student activities and campus tours. One possibility is to divide the new students into smaller groups, with two trained student volunteers leading each group. This arrangement may make it easier for students to ask questions and become acquainted with one another. The tour could include the library, health center, counseling center, sports facilities, dining areas, and the main academic buildings.

I would also like to propose a short informal activity after the official presentations. For example, we could organize a student-club fair or a welcome reception where new students can meet representatives from different organizations. However, this would affect the budget and require additional space, so we should discuss whether it is realistic.

Regarding the agenda, I suggest that we allow at least twenty minutes for volunteer recruitment and training. In previous years, some volunteers received their instructions too late and were uncertain about their responsibilities. We should establish a clear application process, training date, dress code, and communication channel.

I will bring a draft tour route, a list of possible activities, and an estimate of the number of volunteers needed. I also support inviting Rachel Green, because international students may require additional assistance during registration and campus tours.

I look forward to discussing these points with everyone.
