I am writing to suggest that we arrange a meeting to discuss the student orientation program for the coming academic year. Since the program will involve several departments, I think it would be helpful to meet early and establish the main objectives, responsibilities, and deadlines.

Would Tuesday, August 4, from 10:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. be convenient for you? I have reserved Meeting Room 302 in the Administration Building for that time, although we could also hold the meeting online if some participants cannot attend in person.
